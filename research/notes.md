### Practical checks performed on the concept

- Common core excluded from the unit footprint and ceiling.
- Direct routes from entrance to bedroom group and living spaces; ensuite reached through dressing room.
- Main bed module 1.5 × 2m; two single-bed modules; wardrobe and desk footprints drawn in both renderers.
- Main table-side route about 1.04m; worktop-to-table clearance about 1.03m under model assumptions. Dining seats are on the living-room side to keep the kitchen working space clear.
- Third bedroom bed turned across the rear; desk repositioned to clear the door swing. Second bedroom wardrobe moved to the side wall to avoid its doorway.
- Tests check furniture/fixture overlap, enclosure, modeled door sweeps and camera standing points. They do not certify structural feasibility, accessibility, ventilation, fire egress or building-code compliance. Some decorative geometry is outside these simplified footprints.
- Internal kitchen/bathrooms remain a daylight/ventilation tradeoff. Structural walls, original slabs, risers, core connections and service-area accounting require actual project drawings.
